Would a quality filter remove them?

Length and mean-quality filters (Filtlong, chopper, NanoFilt, fastplong) score the whole read. A chimera made of two good molecules is a long, high-quality read. The table shows how many reads in each class pass common settings, based on dorado's mean read Q-score.
